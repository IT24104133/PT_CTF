EXHIBIT D · CASE FILESOLSTICE DYNAMICS · SECURITY

You're deeper into the system now, using login details that were never meant to be yours. You find two more weak spots. The "bulk employee import" feature accepts XML files without checking them carefully. It turns out you can trick an XML file into asking for things it shouldn't — like files and secrets stored on the server.

Then there's the payslip system. It trusts a simple number in the web address to decide what you can see. Change the number, and it never checks who you really are.

THAHANI · DIRECTOR OF SECURITY "Payslips," Thahani says slowly, reading over your shoulder. "He wasn't only after the Meridian Engine, was he? He was looking for something to use against someone. Someone inside the company."

The payslip that gets exposed belongs to Inchaf, a payroll clerk in Finance. At first, nothing about him looks unusual — quiet, ten years at the company. But his payslip shows he owes a lot of money. That's exactly the kind of thing someone could use to pressure him.

This is the first clue that this wasn't only an outside job. Someone inside Solstice Dynamics might have been a target too.
