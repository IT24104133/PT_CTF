CASE BRIEFINGSOLSTICE DYNAMICS · SECURITY

At 6:42 on a Tuesday morning, Solstice Dynamics' security system noticed something strange. There was a sudden spike of failed login attempts on the HR portal. Twenty minutes later, someone logged in successfully as an admin — from an IP address nobody knew.

By the time Thahani, the Director of Security, saw the alert on her phone, the attacker was already gone. There was no ransom note. Nothing was destroyed. It was a quiet, careful break-in — and that made Thahani worry even more.

Solstice Dynamics' most valuable asset is the Meridian Engine, special software that controls shipping routes for half the company's clients. If a rival company got this software, it would be worth millions. Thahani already has one name in mind: a former contractor who left the company under strange circumstances eighteen months ago. Online, he goes only by a codename — Ghost_Fen.

She doesn't have enough proof to call the police yet. What she has is a hacked server, a strong feeling, and you — the newest member of her incident-response team, fresh out of training, about to be given a real case.

THAHANI · DIRECTOR OF SECURITY “I need someone to go back to the start,” she tells you. “I want to know every step he took. How he got in. What he touched. And most of all, I want proof it was him.”

This case is now yours.
